Official scoring framework

How IPL players are ranked

All leaderboard views use one shared scoring engine and the supplied IPL dataset.

Coverage-aware scoring

Each metric is normalized among players in its role with robust 5th–95th percentile bounds. Lower-is-better metrics are inverted. Minimum samples and shrinkage toward a neutral value reduce small-sample extremes.

Unavailable metrics remain unavailable. They are never imputed as zero or full marks. A category score is reduced by the share of its expected metrics supported by data, and missing category weight is not reassigned to other categories.

The final score uses the six weighted categories and the role-specific rate component. Consistency, Pressure Performance, and Match Impact are separate indicators and are not added to the final score.

Observed source data

Player batting and bowling events, required run rates, wickets in hand, innings/over state, pace or spin, batter handedness, match results, match stages, match dates, and recorded Player-of-the-Match IDs.

Derived estimates include close finishes, last-over outcomes, top-four opponent strength, home/away balance, multi-season consistency, batting-position diversity, and expensive spells.

Unavailable in the source

Fielder-attributed catches and run-outs, stumpings by keeper, dropped catches, runs saved, pitch conditions, and must-win labels. These remain unscored and are identified in profile metric evidence.

The published coverage is 2022–2026 IPL only; it is not an all-time IPL leaderboard.
